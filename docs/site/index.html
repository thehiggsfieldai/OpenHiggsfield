Keys stay on your server and are decrypted for authorized provider calls. Saved secrets are not returned to the browser. This is not zero-knowledge storage.

A FEW STRAIGHT ANSWERS

Before you
hit build.

Is this an official Higgsfield product?

No. OpenHiggsfield is an independent community project, not affiliated with or endorsed by Higgsfield AI, TypeSafe or the other providers it supports.

Is everything free?

The studio is free and MIT-licensed. AI providers bill your connected accounts, and self-hosting requires infrastructure. Review provider pricing before generation.

Can I try it without self-hosting?

Explore TheHiggsField.ai and join the hosted private-beta waitlist. Joining the waitlist does not create a studio account.

Does it support every Higgsfield feature?

No. The current adapters cover selected Wan and Seedance video workflows and Qwen image editing. Check the repository for current capabilities and restrictions.

Can I build a SaaS with this?

Yes, under the MIT license. You are responsible for your deployment, security, provider terms, user support and any billing system you add.
